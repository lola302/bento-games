# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Anyone with a phone and four spare minutes, teens through adults: students on a bus or a school Chromebook, adults on a lunch break, people in bed right after midnight when the new box drops. They play a few compartments, compare with friends in a group chat, and come back tomorrow.

## Product Purpose
Bento Games: one app with seven daily mini games (Outbid, Unpopular Opinion, Blind Rank, Guesstimate, Memeline, Blitz, Rebus). Every day is a fresh "bento box" that's the same for everyone. Each game has a shareable result, streaks, and an archive unlocked by a one-time Bento Pass. Success = people finish several compartments a day, come back tomorrow without being reminded, and paste results into chats.

## Brand Personality
Colorful, handmade, playful. A colorful analog journal: pastel paper pages, ballpoint ink, washi tape, highlighter swipes, rubber stamps, doodles drawn by hand. Friendly to every age: funny, never mean, never crude.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS UI (gray cards, thin borders, blue accents) and "classic" chunky game UI.
- Emoji stuck in boxes as icons. Icons and logos are drawn doodles.
- Dotted/grid backgrounds.
- Ad-cluttered "unblocked games" portals; casino-style shouting.
- Copying another game's look (Wordle tiles verbatim, Krillion's ocean theme).

## Design Principles
1. **Play in one tap.** The puzzle is on screen immediately; no signup, no splash.
2. **Every result is a brag.** A funny rank title and an emoji grid worth pasting.
3. **One journal, many pages.** Shared layout and behavior; each game owns a marker color and a doodle.
4. **Handmade, not messy.** Wobbly edges and tape add warmth; type, spacing and contrast stay clean and readable.
5. **Honest numbers and images.** Sample crowd data is labeled; photos are freely licensed and credited.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Never color-only feedback: marks also use words, ticks or crosses. Full keyboard play, visible focus, 44px+ touch targets, reduced-motion support, inputs at 16px+ so phones don't zoom.
